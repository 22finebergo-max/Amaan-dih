How Hire Amaans works
  • Choose any of 8 diverse Amaan appearances and any of 6 jobs—48 possible combinations.
  • Heritage changes only the portrait. Job and level determine every stat and bonus.
  • Power strengthens job-building synergy; Speed shortens shifts; Efficiency improves XP and Hire Token pay.
  • Levels 10 and 20 unlock silver and gold uniforms.

  • Hired Amaans work offline for up to 8 hours and survive every prestige reset.

    How Pet Amaans work
    • Egg odds are exactly 50%, 30%, 15%, 4.9% and 0.1% per hatch.
    • Amaan Dih is the Mythic 0.1% pet.
    • At 5 Halos, equipped pets earn XP from completed Hire Amaans shifts and every 100 manual clicks.
    • Feed a pet with amaans for 50 XP. Feed cost scales with its level and your production.
    • Two duplicate copies can be Breed/Fused into a Bond Star, making that pet's boost 15% stronger. Maximum 5 stars.
    • Each pet has a cooldown ability. It must be equipped to activate it.
    • Pets, levels, duplicates, Bond Stars, cooldowns and equipped slots all survive Transcendence.

    Balance update

    Gold Star production softcaps at ×100; Diploma production at ×25. Above each threshold, further growth uses a 0.35 power. Combined global bonuses softcap at ×1,000. Gold Star earning multipliers softcap at ×10 and Diploma earning at ×5. Output above 1e30 per click or second also has diminishing returns. Click Frenzy is ×25, and the 8/13-Halo milestone boosts are +25% each. Currency totals are capped at 1e100 to prevent numeric overflow. Your earned currencies are retained unless above that safety ceiling.
